Method and limits

The estimated unit catalog price divides the dated public catalog price by one explicit pack quantity parsed from the product title. Catalog cost per cube divides that estimate by listed length × width × height in cubic feet. The family index centers each buyer-facing family at 100.

This is not a current-price promise, supplier-cost table, margin model, landed-cost calculation, freight quote, inventory commitment, or competitor comparison. It contains no competitor pricing and cannot support a relative price or savings conclusion.
